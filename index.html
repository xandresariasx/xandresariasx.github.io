About me

Andres is a distinguished researcher and engineer in the fields of image analysis, machine learning, and data analysis. His research focuses on developing and applying of methods for the analysis of large-scale medical, epidemiological, and clinical trials data.

He has worked as researcher and leadership positions at prestigious research centers and hospitals such as ZIB Berlin, Moffit Cancer Center, and Erasmus MC Medical Center.

Andres has a broad background in statistics, computer science, machine learning, deep learning, and medical image analysis.
